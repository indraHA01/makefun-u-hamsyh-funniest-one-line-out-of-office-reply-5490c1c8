# Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply

A make.fun bounty entry for [Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-out-of-office-reply-5490c1c8).

## The entry

> I'm out of office — my auto-reply is covering for me and it's already up for a promotion.

## Why it's funny

The joke turns the out-of-office message on itself: the auto-reply isn't just holding the fort, it has taken over the job — and is already doing it well enough to earn a promotion. No beach, no overflowing inbox, no "limited access to email": the reply itself is the overachieving employee, and its owner is the one who looks replaceable. It lands on the first read and still reads like a message a real person would gladly switch on before going away.
